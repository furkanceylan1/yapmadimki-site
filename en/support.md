# Support

Found a problem or have an idea? Write to us: [yapmadimki.app@gmail.com](mailto:yapmadimki.app@gmail.com)

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I unlock Premium on another device?**
On a device signed in with the same Apple account, open Settings › Restore purchases in the App.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
On your iPhone, go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions and choose Yapmadım Ki!. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

**Can I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>

**How do I bring back a card I hid?**
In the App, open Settings › Hidden cards and bring items back one by one or all at once.

**Why are my custom packs missing on another device?**
Custom packs are stored only on the device where you wrote them; they are not synced.

**Does the App need the internet?**
No. You only need a connection to buy or restore Premium.
