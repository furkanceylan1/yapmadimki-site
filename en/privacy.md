# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

**In short: Yapmadım Ki! collects no personal data from you.** There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## 1. What we do not collect
The App does not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers or usage statistics. It uses no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools and does not track you across apps or websites.

## 2. What stays on your device
To make the game work, the following is stored only on your device:
- your favorite cards and the cards and forfeits you hide,
- the custom packs you write,
- your language, your last pack selections and similar preferences.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the App deletes all of it.

## 3. Purchases
Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The App only reads Apple's purchase records on your device to know whether Premium is active. For Apple's practices, see <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## 4. Network
The App connects only to Apple's App Store servers, for purchases and restoring them. It sends no data to any other server.

## 5. Children
The App is for ages 13 and up and collects no personal data from users of any age.

## 6. GDPR and KVKK
Because we neither collect nor process personal data, we hold no data about you under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). You can send any questions about your rights to the address below.

## 7. Changes
If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.
